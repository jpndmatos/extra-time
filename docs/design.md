# Touchline goals-only hub

The application is a single full-width goal feed and player. Every goal returned by the provider is available without a favourite-team catalogue, match selection or setup screens. Main defaults to senior games; Women, U21, U19, Other youth and Reserves split the remaining clips by team names, post titles and fixture competition metadata. When posts omit classification metadata, they remain in Main. Old view, team, match and feed query parameters are ignored and removed from the canonical URL. Category tabs and the All goals / Unseen / Seen dropdown sit at the left of a balanced header. Search is centred and blends into the bar; spoilers use a labelled icon button. Refresh and connection states sit at the far right. Top-right source indicators open individual connection states, last successful fetch times and refresh details; public Reddit RSS is labelled separately from the OAuth API.

Poppins is locally hosted. Neutral surfaces distinguish unseen and seen goals. Muted burnt orange identifies unwatched rows, deep green identifies the active clip, and subtle rules separate compact records. Minute, team names, scores and scorer use aligned columns. Crest aliases bridge provider naming differences, and youth/women’s teams inherit the available parent-club image without changing their identities. Search accepts aliases and accents while protecting hidden scorer details.

Opening a goal updates its seen state immediately and saves in the background. On phones, the player mounts only when opened and unloads on Back to goals. Spoiler-protected clips count after reveal. Manual mark-as-unwatched stays respected. Previous/Next follows the visible results and keeps the selected row in view. Cinema is available on desktop.

Retain source attribution, metadata resolution, mirror recovery, live server events, and loading/error/empty/full states. Sample mode is explicitly fictional and supplies no footage. No banners, catalogue limits or fixture configuration block the real feed. Legacy backend preferences and import endpoints remain compatible; the UI does not expose or depend on favourites.
