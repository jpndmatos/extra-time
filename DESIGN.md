# Extra Time

A quiet football viewing desk, with a small reference to match clocks and old scoreboards.

- Poppins throughout. Tabular numerals align scores and minutes. Pixel geometry belongs to the clock mark; reading text stays smooth.
- Neutral charcoal surfaces form the header, player information and footer. Footage remains on black. Divisions use the same subtle rule colour.
- Muted sage green belongs only to unseen clips in the goal list. Player controls, same-match clips and connection indicators use neutral greys. Seen clips and their checkmarks use neutral charcoal, including while selected. Muted orange identifies the current minute and noteworthy goal tags, with a small orange edge linking the selected feed row to the selected same-match clip.
- Controls use the same four-pixel corners and hover treatment above and below the video. The logo stays borderless. Phone targets remain 44 pixels.
- Keep navigation and player controls in place. Preserve the feed scroll and mark a clip seen when the viewer leaves it.
- Keep brand colours in tokens.css. Keep responsive workspace geometry in workspace.css. Do not add ornamental sections, gradients or retro fonts to the footage interface.
